Before you travel

Small things that make the day easier.

  • Keep your ticket handySave or print your digital ticket and show it at the terminal.
  • Add a backup numberNgutees tickets carry a second phone number so someone can be reached.
  • Declare luggage when bookingCharges are separate from your fare and confirmed by staff.
  • Name the receiver for goodsGive the receiver phone number so collection is easy.
  • Plan VIP and charter earlyAsk for your date as soon as you know it.
  • Call if plans changeUse the numbers on the Contact page for help before or during your trip.

About Ngutees

Ngutees Express is bringing its existing transport experience into a better digital journey.

Who we are

Ngutees Express Limited is an interstate passenger transport company. We run VIP bus travel and charter services, with our head office in Gboko, Benue State.

Our journey

Ngutees Express Limited is based in Gboko, Benue State, with terminals and operations in Lagos and Benin. Its vehicles include standard minibuses and VIP minibuses, and it serves eight destinations. The founding story and year will be added once Ngutees confirms them.

Our services

Passenger travel, VIP travel, charter services, and goods sent by waybill.

Our network

Routes from Gboko to Lagos, Abuja, Benin, Port Harcourt, Jos, Enugu, Onitsha and Asaba. Operations and terminals in Gboko, Lagos and Benin.

Our commitment

Your journey. Our commitment. Clear booking, a ticket you can show, and people to call when you need help.

The digital roadmap

This prototype is Phase 1. Each phase connects more of the real operation.

  1. 1Website and prototypeRoutes, contact, fleet, terminals, booking, ticket and waybill prototypes.
  2. 2Real bookingBooking backend, seat inventory, payment gateway, e-ticket database, customer accounts.
  3. 3OperationsWaybill management, operational dashboard, journey updates, customer notifications.
  4. 4Live trackingGPS and fleet integration, real-time journey tracking, passenger notifications, analytics.
